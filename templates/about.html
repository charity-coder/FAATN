About FAATN

Federation of Association of Artisans and Technicians of Nigeria

Who We Are

The Federation of Association of Artisans and Technicians of Nigeria (FAATN) is a nationally registered non-governmental, non-profit organisation established in 2017 and incorporated under the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC Registration No. CAC/IT/103696).

FAATN provides a structured national platform for artisans and technicians. We advance indigenous technology, strengthen technical competence, foster innovation, and enable structured collaboration among practitioners, tertiary institutions, industry, government agencies, and development partners.

Our mandate is to position Nigeria’s artisan and technical sector as a central contributor to industrial growth, employment, technology transfer, economic diversification, and national development.

01

🎯 Vision

To be the leading national institution advancing innovation, indigenous technology, and sustainable development through Nigeria’s artisans and technicians.

02

🚀 Mission

To organise, equip, and empower artisans and technicians through professional training, innovation programmes, strategic partnerships, technology transfer, renewable energy initiatives, ethical enterprise standards, research collaboration, and reliable access to modern tools and market opportunities.

03

🇳🇬 Our Commitment

To support the Federal Government’s industrialisation agenda by strengthening technical capacity, promoting local manufacturing, expanding decent employment, and establishing Nigerian artisans and technicians as drivers of national development and global competitiveness.

A National Platform for Skill, Innovation, and Prosperity

The Federation of Association of Artisans and Technicians of Nigeria was established on a clear conviction: that the professionals who build, fabricate, install, repair, and innovate are essential to Nigeria’s industrial future. For too long, this community has lacked a unified national platform. FAATN was created to close that gap.

Our vision places indigenous technical capacity at the centre of industrialisation, employment generation, technology transfer, and sustainable development. Through FAATN-GATE, we connect People → Skills → Innovation → Opportunities → Impact in structured partnership with tertiary institutions, industry, government, and development organisations.

I invite artisans, technicians, engineers, policymakers, and investors to engage with FAATN. Together, we will strengthen Nigeria’s technical foundation and expand pathways to enterprise, dignity, and national prosperity.

Professor Mazi Ojiaku
Chairman, FAATN

Institutional Commitment, Programmes, and Partnership

FAATN is an organised institution with defined programmes in skills development, training and certification, innovation support, renewable energy readiness, research collaboration, and market linkage. These programmes are delivered with transparency, accountability, and respect for every stakeholder.

We welcome artisans and technicians seeking professional growth; engineers and institutions seeking technical partnership; and government, industry, and development partners committed to a stronger Nigerian technical sector. FAATN-GATE provides a practical national entry point for registration, collaboration, and service delivery.

We invite formal engagement with FAATN in the service of national development.

Barrister Ajoku Philip Ogadinma
Secretary, FAATN
