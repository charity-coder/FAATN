About FAATN

Federation of Artisans and Technicians of Nigeria

Who We Are

The Federation of Artisans and Technicians of Nigeria (FAATN) is a nationally registered non-governmental and non-profit organisation established in 2017 and incorporated under CAC Registration No. CAC/IT/103696.

FAATN was founded to provide a national platform for artisans and technicians, promote indigenous technology development, strengthen technical skills, encourage innovation, and facilitate collaboration among artisans, technicians, tertiary institutions, industries, government agencies, and development partners.

The Federation is committed to transforming Nigeria’s artisan and technical subsector into a major contributor to industrial development, employment generation, technology transfer, economic diversification, and national prosperity.

01

🎯 Vision

To become the foremost organisation promoting innovation, indigenous technology, and sustainable development through Nigerian artisans and technicians.

02

🚀 Mission

To mobilise, organise and empower artisans and technicians through training, innovation, strategic partnerships, technology transfer, renewable energy development, artificial intelligence, business ethics, research collaboration, and access to modern tools and opportunities.

03

🇳🇬 Our Commitment

Supporting the Federal Government’s industrialisation agenda by promoting innovation, strengthening technical capacity, encouraging local manufacturing, creating employment opportunities, and positioning Nigerian artisans and technicians as key drivers of national development and global competitiveness.

A national platform for skill, innovation and prosperity

The Federation of Artisans and Technicians of Nigeria was founded on a simple conviction: that the men and women who build, fabricate, install, repair and innovate with their hands are indispensable to Nigeria’s industrial future. For too long, this community has operated without a unified national platform. FAATN exists to change that.

Our vision is to place indigenous technical capacity at the centre of industrialisation, employment generation, technology transfer and sustainable development. Through FAATN-GATE, we are connecting People → Skills → Innovation → Opportunities → Impact in partnership with tertiary institutions, industry, government and development partners.

I call on artisans, technicians, engineers, policymakers and investors to join us. Together, we will strengthen Nigeria’s technical backbone and open pathways to dignity, enterprise and national prosperity.

Professor Mazi Ojiaku
Chairman, FAATN

Institutional commitment, programmes and partnership

FAATN is not only a vision; it is an organised institution. We are committed to clear programmes in skills development, training and certification pathways, innovation support, renewable energy readiness, research collaboration and market linkage—delivered with transparency, accountability and respect for every stakeholder who engages with us.

Our doors are open to artisans and technicians seeking growth; to engineers and institutions offering technical partnership; and to government, industry and development partners who share the goal of a stronger Nigerian technical subsector. FAATN-GATE provides a practical entry point for registration, collaboration and service.

We invite you to work with us—formally, productively and in the national interest.

Barrister Ajoku Philip Ogadinma
Secretary, FAATN
