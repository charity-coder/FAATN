01 · Vision

🎯 Our Vision

To become the foremost organisation promoting innovation, indigenous technology, and sustainable development through Nigerian artisans and technicians.

02 · Mission

🚀 Our Mission

To mobilise, organise and empower artisans and technicians through training, innovation, strategic partnerships, technology transfer, renewable energy development, artificial intelligence, business ethics, research collaboration, and access to modern tools and opportunities.

03 · Commitment

🇳🇬 Our Commitment

Supporting the Federal Government’s industrialisation agenda by promoting innovation, strengthening technical capacity, encouraging local manufacturing, creating employment, and positioning Nigerian artisans and technicians as key drivers of national development.
