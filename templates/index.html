01 · Vision

🎯 Our Vision

To become the foremost organisation promoting innovation, indigenous technology, and sustainable development through Nigerian artisans and technicians.

02 · Mission

🚀 Our Mission

To mobilise, organise and empower artisans and technicians through training, innovation, strategic partnerships, technology transfer, renewable energy development, artificial intelligence, business ethics, research collaboration, and access to modern tools and opportunities.

03 · Commitment

🇳🇬 Our Commitment

Supporting the Federal Government’s industrialisation agenda by promoting innovation, strengthening technical capacity, encouraging local manufacturing, creating employment, and positioning Nigerian artisans and technicians as key drivers of national development.

Help

Frequently asked questions

What is FAATN-GATE?

FAATN-GATE is the digital gateway of the Federation of Artisans and Technicians of Nigeria. It connects people, skills, innovation, opportunities and impact for artisans, technicians, engineers, institutions, government and industry.

Who can register on FAATN-GATE?

Artisans, technicians, engineers and technical professionals, institutions, government and development partners, industry, innovators, opportunity providers, and organisations that want to partner with FAATN.

Is registration free?

Yes. Creating a FAATN-GATE account is free. After you register and log in, you can access the member dashboard, directory, training and opportunity pathways.

How do I enter the GATE?

Open the Gateway page, choose the option that describes you (for example artisan, technician or engineer), then create a free account or log in to your dashboard.

How can I contact FAATN?

Green Station, 5 Amiyi/Hospital Road, Umuaka, Njaba LGA, Imo State. Tel: +234 915 496 8109 · +234 803 677 1117. Email: faatnigeria@gmail.com. Social: @faatnigeria.

What is FAATN’s CAC registration number?

FAATN is incorporated under CAC Registration No. CAC/IT/103696 and was established in 2017.
